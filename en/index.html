English, German, French

The app speaks your iPhone’s language. Switch between several TVs with a swipe.

On your wrist.

With the Apple Watch, your iPhone stays in your pocket. The crown controls the volume, a swipe takes you to power, channel and your apps.

Which TVs?

The TV and the iPhone must be on the same Wi‑Fi. On first connection the TV asks once to “Allow”, after that everything just works.

Works

Samsung Smart TV

With Tizen, roughly from 2016 models on. Tested with a 2025 Samsung QLED. To turn the TV on over Wi‑Fi, enable “Power On with Mobile” (Settings › General › Network › Expert Settings).

In preparation

LG webOS, Sony Bravia and Roku

Already built in, coming in an update once tested on real devices.

Not yet

Others

Hisense, Philips, Google TV, Android TV and Fire TV are not supported yet.

Good to know

Apps belong to their providers

The apps run on your TV. Teek Taike only starts them and is not affiliated with the manufacturers or app providers.

Your data stays with you.

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app talks directly to your TV on your Wi‑Fi.

What is stored

Only on your iPhone: the name and address of your TVs and the permission the TV grants when you tap “Allow”. Remove the TV or delete the app, and everything is gone.

The only connection to the internet

For app icons, Teek Taike asks Apple’s public App Store search for the app’s name, for example “Netflix”. No data about you is sent.
