Features

Just like a real remote.

Only nicer, and always with you. Everything you need in the evening on one screen, no scrolling. The whole remote is free.

Keys you can feel

Volume and channel are long rocker keys that tilt when pressed. The D-pad gives way and every key clicks.

Apps with one tap

Start your streaming apps and every other app on your TV. Your favorites sit in the bar at the bottom.

Now playing

The top of the app shows which app is running on your TV right now. Checked sparingly, only while the app is open.

D-pad, touchpad, numbers

One switch right above the D-pad: press arrows, swipe with your finger or type channel numbers. Everything in the same place, nothing jumps.

iPhone buttons as volume

While the app is open, the iPhone’s volume buttons control the TV.

Siri and Shortcuts

“Volume up with Teek Taike”, “Turn off the TV with Teek Taike”. Also in Control Center and as a sleep timer with an automation.

A connection that holds

When you open the app, it connects to the TV that is on. A Wi‑Fi watcher notices when the network drops for a moment and reconnects by itself.

Connection at a glance

A dot next to your TV’s name shows whether everything is connected. If something is wrong, tap it and the app tells you why.

Several phones at home

Every iPhone in the family gets its own permission on the TV. Tap “Allow” once per phone, and the devices stop kicking each other out.

Game on with one tap

TV on, right HDMI input, the volume you like: one tap on “Play” does it all. “Back to TV” returns to regular programs.

Play time for kids

Set 45 minutes, get a notification 5 minutes before, then the TV switches back to regular programs or turns off.

English, German, French

The app speaks your iPhone’s language.

Live, movies, series

Categories, favorites and recently watched, plus one search field for everything. Name, color and sort your folders yourself.

Channel list like on your TV

Live channels in a list, just like on your TV, with what is on right now. Swipe to change channel.

TV guide with folder menu

The guide for all channels as a grid. Pick the folder at the top, and if your provider has an archive, catch up on missed shows.

Folders with one tap

Pin your most important folders to the top. Your channels are always one tap away.

Your channel order

Combine playlists

Several of your own playlists can merge into one view if you like. Each one stays as it is, and you can undo the link at any time.

Add a playlist the easy way

No need to type long addresses on your phone: scan a QR code, tap a link or send the playlist from a computer on your Wi‑Fi.

Send to TV

Your Samsung plays the stream itself, and the iPhone becomes a remote with a touchpad. Everything stays on your home Wi‑Fi.

Multi-view

Two or four channels at once, sound from the one you tap. Also on the TV with screen mirroring.

My List and Continue watching

Save movies and series for later. A row shows where you left off, and at the end of an episode the next one counts down.

Ratings at a glance

The rating sits right on the poster. Sort by rating or show only titles rated 7 ★ and up.

Movie and series pages with trailer

A large image or trailer, description, rating and cast, as far as your provider supplies them. Then play, or continue where you were.

Like a streaming app

Double tap to skip, swipe to change channel, adjust brightness and volume at the edges. With AirPlay and picture in picture.

Screen lock and parental lock

Lock the screen in the player so no accidental tap stops the movie. Adult categories are locked, and you can protect your own folders with a PIN.

Empty and clean

Teek Taike contains no channels, movies, series or playlists and does not refer you to any provider. You only play what you have lawfully obtained yourself.

TVs

Which TVs?

The TV and the iPhone must be on the same Wi‑Fi. On first connection the TV asks once to “Allow”, after that everything just works.

Works

Samsung Smart TV

With Tizen, roughly from 2016 models on. Tested with a 2025 Samsung QLED. To turn the TV on over Wi‑Fi, enable “Power On with Mobile” (Settings › General › Network › Expert Settings).

FAQ

Good to know.

How much is Pro?

The remote is free. Teek Taike Pro is a one-time purchase in the App Store, no subscription and no hidden costs. The app shows the exact price in your currency before you buy.

Are channels included?

No. Teek Taike contains no channels, movies, series or playlists and does not refer you to any provider. The player only plays your own playlists that you have lawfully obtained, as an M3U link or a login with username.

Several phones in one household?

Sure. Every iPhone registers with the TV under its own name, and the TV asks “Allow?” once per phone. After that, everyone controls the same TV without kicking each other out. Pro works on all devices with your Apple Account.

Which TVs work?

Samsung Smart TVs with Tizen, roughly from 2016 models on. The TV and the iPhone must be on the same Wi‑Fi. More brands will come in an update once tested on real devices.

How does Send to TV work?

Tap Send in the player and your Samsung plays the stream itself. The iPhone becomes the remote. If your provider allows only one connection, the iPhone fetches the stream and passes it on to the TV over your Wi‑Fi. The app stays open for this.

Where are my login details stored?

Only on your iPhone. Your playlist’s password is kept in the iOS keychain. There is no account with us, no ads and no tracking.

Privacy

Your data stays with you.

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app talks directly to your TV on your Wi‑Fi.

What is stored

Only on your iPhone: the name and address of your TVs and the permission the TV grants when you tap “Allow”. Remove the TV or delete the app, and everything is gone.

Your login details

The link, server and username of your playlists stay on your iPhone, the password is kept in the keychain. Playback runs directly between your iPhone and your provider, not through us.

Sending stays at home

When you send to the TV, everything runs on your Wi‑Fi. If the iPhone has to pass the stream on, only your TV can fetch it, through a random address that disappears when you stop watching.

Purchase through Apple

You buy Pro in the App Store. Apple handles the payment, we never see your name or payment details.

Connections to the internet

For app icons, the app asks Apple’s public App Store search. Trailers only load when you open a movie or series page.

No content included

Teek Taike comes with no channels or playlists. You decide which playlist of your own you add.
