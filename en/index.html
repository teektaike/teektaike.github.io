Teek Taike means “press on it”

Press it.
Your TV responds.

Teek Taike turns your iPhone and Apple Watch into a remote. Keys that feel like real keys, your apps on the big screen with one tap, and a player for your own playlists.

Does my TV work?See more

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Just like a real remote.

Only nicer, and always with you. Everything you need in the evening on one screen, no scrolling.

Keys you can feel

Volume and channel are long rocker keys that tilt when pressed. The D-pad gives way and every key clicks.

Apps with one tap

Start your streaming apps and every other app on your TV. Your favorites sit in the bar at the bottom.

iPhone buttons as volume

While the app is open, the iPhone’s volume buttons control the TV.

Siri and Shortcuts

“Volume up with Teek Taike”, “Turn off the TV with Teek Taike”. Also in Control Center and as a sleep timer with an automation.

Touchpad and keyboard

For search in apps and the TV’s browser: type instead of picking letters one by one.

English, German, French

The app speaks your iPhone’s language. Switch between several TVs with a swipe.

The right TV, right away

When you open the app, it looks for the TV that is on and answers fastest and connects to it. You can turn this off in the settings.

Game on with one tap

TV on, right HDMI input, the volume you like: one tap on “Play” does it all. “Back to TV” returns to regular programs. Also with Siri: “Play with Teek Taike”.

Play time for kids

Set 45 minutes, get a notification 5 minutes before, then the TV switches back to regular programs or turns off.

A player for your own playlists.

Add your IPTV playlist as an M3U link or with your provider’s server, username and password. Live, movies and series are sorted into three sections.

Live, movies, series

Categories, favorites and recently watched. Name, color and sort your folders yourself. For live channels you see what is on now.

Like a streaming app

Double tap to skip, swipe to change channel, adjust brightness and volume at the edges. Choose audio and subtitles in their own panel, with AirPlay and picture in picture.

TV guide with catch-up

The guide for all channels as a grid. If your provider has an archive, watch missed shows.

Search everything

One search field for channels, movies and series.

On the big screen

With AirPlay or TV mode: the TV shows only the video, the iPhone becomes a remote with a touchpad.

Multi-view

Two or four channels at once, sound from the one you tap.

Parental lock

Adult categories are locked, lock your own folders with a PIN.

Empty and clean

Teek Taike contains no channels, movies, series or playlists and does not refer you to any provider. You only play what you have lawfully obtained yourself.

On your wrist.

With the Apple Watch, your iPhone stays in your pocket. The crown controls the volume, a swipe takes you to power, channel and your apps.

Which TVs?

The TV and the iPhone must be on the same Wi‑Fi. On first connection the TV asks once to “Allow”, after that everything just works.

Works

Samsung Smart TV

With Tizen, roughly from 2016 models on. Tested with a 2025 Samsung QLED. To turn the TV on over Wi‑Fi, enable “Power On with Mobile” (Settings › General › Network › Expert Settings).

In preparation

More brands

More TVs will come in an update once tested on real devices.

Works

Game consoles via HDMI

Teek Taike switches your Samsung to your console’s HDMI input. The console itself is not controlled.

Good to know

Apps belong to their providers

The apps run on your TV. Teek Taike only starts them and is not affiliated with the manufacturers or app providers.

Your data stays with you.

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The app talks directly to your TV on your Wi‑Fi.

What is stored

Only on your iPhone: the name and address of your TVs and the permission the TV grants when you tap “Allow”. Remove the TV or delete the app, and everything is gone.

The only connection to the internet

For app icons, Teek Taike asks Apple’s public App Store search for the app’s name. No data about you is sent.

Your playlists

The link, server and username of your playlists stay on your iPhone, the password is kept in the keychain. Playback runs directly between your iPhone and your provider, not through us.
