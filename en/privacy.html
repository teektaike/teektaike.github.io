Privacy Policy

For the Teek Taike app (iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch) and this website. Last updated: 17 November 2026. In case of doubt, the German version applies.

In short

Teek Taike has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app talks directly to your TV on your Wi‑Fi. As the provider, we receive no data about you from the app.

2. The app

Connection to the TV. The app finds TVs on your Wi‑Fi and sends them key presses and app launches. While the app is open, it asks the TV which app is running right now. This connection runs directly between your iPhone and your TV. It does not go through our servers, we don’t have any.

What is stored on your iPhone. The name, network address and hardware address (MAC) of your TVs, the permission the TV grants when you tap “Allow”, your choice of app tiles, learned key sequences and your settings. This data stays on your device. It is removed when you remove the TV in the settings or delete the app.

App icons. To show the tiles with the right icons, the app asks Apple’s public App Store search for an app’s name, and downloads the icon. Apple receives the search request and your internet connection’s IP address. No data about you or your TV is sent. Apple is responsible for this processing, under Apple’s privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

Player for your own playlists. When you add a playlist, the app stores its name, link or server address and username on your iPhone, and the password in the iOS keychain. It also remembers your favorites, My List, recently watched items, where you paused a movie, pinned folders and your folder settings. All of this stays on your device. When loading the playlist and during playback, your iPhone connects directly to your provider’s server. Your provider receives your IP address and your login details, as with any other player app. Your provider is responsible for this processing. We receive none of this data. Teek Taike contains no content and does not refer you to any provider.

Send to TV. When you send a stream to your TV, the app gives the TV the stream’s address and the TV fetches it from your provider itself. If the provider allows only one connection, your iPhone fetches the stream and passes it on to the TV over your Wi‑Fi. For this, the app opens an address on your local network for the duration of playback. It can only be reached over Wi‑Fi, only your TV may fetch it, it contains a random key and it disappears when you stop playback. The data does not leave your home network, except on the way between your provider and your iPhone.

Multi-view and AirPlay. When you show multi-view or a video on the TV with Screen Mirroring or AirPlay, iOS handles the transfer. It runs directly between your devices.

Trailers. When you open a movie or series page that has a trailer, the app loads it through YouTube’s embedded player in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) receives your IP address and technical details about your device. Google is responsible for this processing (policies.google.com/privacy). Nothing is loaded unless you open a movie or series page. The trailer starts when the page opens, and you can hide it there.

Buying Teek Taike Pro. You buy Pro as an in-app purchase through the App Store. Apple handles payment and billing under its own terms. We receive no names and no payment details, only aggregated sales figures from Apple. The app checks on your device whether Pro is unlocked, using Apple’s signed purchase receipt.

Adding a playlist by QR code, link or from a computer. The app reads a QR code with the camera or from a photo you pick. It is evaluated only on your iPhone, and no images are stored. A link to the app shows you what will be added before anything is saved. If you choose “Send from a computer”, the app briefly opens a small page on your Wi‑Fi that accepts a single playlist, and only with the code shown. It closes as soon as you close the window. None of this goes to the internet or to us.

Turning the TV on and play time. To wake the TV from standby, the app sends a wake-up packet to its hardware address on your Wi‑Fi. Play time reminds you with a notification that iOS schedules on your iPhone, without any server.

Local network. On first launch, iOS asks whether the app may find devices on your local network. Without this permission, the app cannot control your TV.

Siri, Shortcuts and Apple Watch. When you use Siri or Shortcuts, Apple processes your voice commands under its own terms. Between Apple Watch and iPhone, only the pressed keys, the list of your TVs and your app tiles are transferred, over Apple’s connection between your own devices.

Crash reports. If you agreed in the iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides us with anonymous crash reports. We cannot identify anyone from them.

3. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics tools, no embedded content and no third-party fonts.

Hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as the IP address, date and time and the page requested, to deliver the website and protect it from attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Email. If you write to us, we use your message and email address only to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them when no longer needed.

4. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). Since the app sends no data to us, we usually hold no data about you. Just write to the address above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.

5. Changes

If the app gains features that process data differently, we will update this policy. The current version on this page applies.
