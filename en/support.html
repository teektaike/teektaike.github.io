Frequently asked questions

My TV is not found.

Turn the TV on, in standby it is often invisible on Wi‑Fi. The iPhone and TV must be on the same Wi‑Fi. On the iPhone, check Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and make sure Teek Taike is on. If needed, enter the IP address manually, you’ll find it on the TV under Settings › Network › Network Status.

The Samsung asks “Allow?”, or I declined by mistake.

On first connection, choose “Allow” with the regular remote. If you declined: on the TV open Settings › General › External Device Manager › Device Connection Manager › Device List, allow or delete Teek Taike and connect again.

The TV doesn’t turn on with the power key.

On the Samsung, enable “Power On with Mobile”: Settings › General › Network › Expert Settings. Some TVs can only be woken over Wi‑Fi for a short time after being turned off.

The iPhone volume buttons do nothing.

They control the TV only while Teek Taike is open. You can turn this on or off in the app under the gear icon › iPhone volume buttons.

How do I use Siri and Control Center?

Say for example “Volume up with Teek Taike” or “Turn off the TV with Teek Taike”. In the Shortcuts app you’ll find all commands under Teek Taike and can add them to Control Center, the Lock Screen or an automation, for example as a sleep timer.

How do I add my playlist to the player?

In the player, tap “Add playlist” and choose M3U or Xtream. If your provider gave you a link, copy it and tap Paste: server, username and password are detected automatically. Teek Taike contains no playlists, you need your own.

My playlist doesn’t load or it says the server blocked me.

Many providers block for a short time when an account logs in too often in a row. The app then waits 10 minutes and shows the saved playlist meanwhile. Tap “Try anyway” to continue right away. If your account is already in use on another device, stop it there, many accounts allow only one connection.

How do I switch to my game console?

Under Console, choose once the HDMI input your console is connected to. After that, one tap is enough. Teek Taike does not control the console itself.

An app doesn’t start on the TV.

The app must be installed on the TV. Teek Taike only starts apps your TV already has.
